Our Legacy

Since 1992: Bridging the Gap

TAS arose from an initiative to address an anticipated market in remote sensing systems resulting from the 1992 Clean Air Act legislation. Originally a spin-off from an independent testing laboratory, Terra Applied Systems was formed to cooperate with industry leaders to field open path FTIR systems for industrial optical remote sensing (ORS) applications.

We are a mission-driven organization. With decades of dedication, our years of experience provide valuable insight for designing, installing, and supporting working systems with emerging technologies. System life cycle support is the TAS difference.
